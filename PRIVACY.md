# Privacy Policy — Chat Limit Auto-Resume

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

**Chat Limit Auto-Resume collects no data.**

- The extension runs only on the AI chat websites listed in its manifest.
- It reads text visible on those pages **locally, in your browser**, solely to detect a usage-limit notice and its wait time. This text is never stored, logged to any server, or transmitted.
- Your settings (auto-click, sound, grace seconds, debug) are saved with the browser's `storage` API on your device only. Countdown state is kept in the page's `sessionStorage` so a reload does not lose it.
- The extension makes **no network requests**, uses no analytics or tracking, loads no remote code, and shares nothing with third parties.

Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored settings.
